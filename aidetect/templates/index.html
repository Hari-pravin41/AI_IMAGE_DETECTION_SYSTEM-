About the assessment

AI Image Detection System provides an image analysis assessment, not a scientific proof of origin. Results may be affected by compression, resizing, screenshots, unusual lighting, editing, and the images used to train the detector.

Visual assessments cannot definitively prove that an image is generated or authentic. Use these results as one supporting signal and verify important evidence through trusted metadata, provenance records, and source review.

Image manipulation and editing detection are not implemented. No accuracy claim or calibrated probability should be inferred from this interface.
